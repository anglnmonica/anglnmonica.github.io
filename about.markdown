I translate between the people who build products and the people who buy them, and I've been an engineer and a writer, so I speak both languages.

I studied computer science engineering and started as a programmer analyst at Cognizant. Later, I helped researchers write papers on LTE, 5G, and Wi-Fi. That's when I realized I enjoyed explaining the technical content more than writing the code.

Since then, I've documented private LTE and 5G networks at Baicells, Ataya, Celona, and now Askey. I've written user guides for 5G NR access points, CPEs, and CloudCore. I've also written network access control (NAC) integration guides that connect Cisco ISE and Aruba ClearPass to Celona Orchestrator, enabling enterprises to authorize devices on their private 5G networks. On the marketing side, I've written white papers for oil fields, warehouses, hospitals, and manufacturing plants. When I can, I test the product myself, either by logging in to a live network remotely or by setting up the hardware at home.

I also spent two years at Intuitive Surgical, testing SureForm staplers on the da Vinci robot and writing white papers to ensure that a fix developed by one instrument team reached the others.

Right now I'm writing the launch content for Askey's private 5G portfolio.


Topics I write about: Private 5G and LTE · CBRS · O-RAN · Neutral Host · AI-Workflow
